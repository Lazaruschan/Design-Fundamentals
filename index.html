Design Fundamentals

SSHD2382. Eighteen guides to the subjects of the course, each with figures and study cues.

Each guide sets out one subject: what it covers, the main ideas, methods you can use, selected figures, and the books behind them. Captions name the book and the page.

How to use. Start from the week you are in: each card below names the weeks its guide supports. At the end of a guide, Study Cues are questions to check what you can now explain. Guides link to one another, and each page ends with the previous and next guide.
